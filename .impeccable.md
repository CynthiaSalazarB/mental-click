## Design Context

### Users
Students (Cynthia's audience) who paste their own course notes to understand a concept, not just
memorize it. Mostly laptop at a desk, sometimes phone. They bring their own API key, so trust
has to be visible.

### Brand Personality
Patient, inky, plainspoken. Feels like a good study notebook, not an app that wants attention.
Emotional goal: calm focus, and the small "oh, I had that wrong" moment.

### Aesthetic Direction
Graph-paper notebook. Cobalt ink on warm paper, hairline grid, no shadows, square corners.
Anti-references: gamified study apps (streaks, XP, confetti), glassy AI-product dashboards,
purple gradients.

**Art-direction thesis:** a graph-paper notebook where the student's wrong idea is written in
pencil and the correction is printed in cobalt ink.

### Costume & Theme (from the design consultation, 2026-09-26)
- Look source: open, designed for it
- Costume: cobalt-grid (References/web-dev-system design-language-library, cluster A), adapted.
  Its fonts (Newsreader, DM Mono) are on impeccable's reflex list, so type was picked fresh:
  Literata (reading serif, built for long on-screen reading) for display, Atkinson Hyperlegible
  Next (legibility-first sans) for body, Kalam (handwriting) only for the student's own voice.
- Theme: light, chosen by Cynthia

### Design Principles
1. The method is the layout: concept, mental model, use case, always in that order, always visible.
2. Try before you see: answers and corrections start hidden; the student commits first.
3. Proof on the page: every evidence quote links back to the exact sentence in their notes.
4. Pencil vs ink: the student's voice (misconception, their guess) is handwriting; verified
   content is print.
5. No reward theatre: no streaks, points, confetti. The reward is understanding.
